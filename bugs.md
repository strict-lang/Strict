- A `for` comprehension whose body calls GPU work is about 20x slower than the same calls in a sequential loop: in StrictComfy's H3ClipEncoder, `for states` returning `norm.Affine(value)` (GroupNorm affines of 5 frame maps of 256x256x128 from conv outputs) took 290-370 ms, while the same five Affine calls in a `for` with a mutable sum took 14 ms. GPU utilization was about 10 % and nsys CPU sampling was mostly kernel time and host copies, as if the comprehension evaluated the deferred conv outputs on host threads. The H3 video VAE encode of 22 frames went from 97 s to 9.7 s by computing the affines in a sequential loop (StrictComfy, 2026-11-15). A related trap: `List(Numbers) + Float32s` concatenates the numbers instead of appending one entry, so building a List(Numbers) step by step needs another way.

Not reproduced since (2026-11-15).
