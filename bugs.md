# Bugs
- The list a GPU result is downloaded into is fresh memory every time: copying 8 MB (a full HD image of 32 bit numbers) into newly committed pages takes 0.86 ms on this PC, into memory used before 0.23 ms (C with VirtualAlloc, i9-13900KF). The copy out of the pinned buffer is a loop over 64 bit words (`strict_words_copy`, a full HD result 1.0 ms, with memcpy 1.3 ms, Mojo's `map_to_host` 0.8 ms), GPU results are no longer searched for NaNs. Reproduce in ../DeltaEngine: `../Strict/strict.exe build Games/FractalZoomer/FractalBench.strict windows`, `cd Games/FractalZoomer/Release/Windows`, `./FractalBench.exe 100 6 1920 1080 5` (max iterations, runs, width, height, mode 5 = `Escape.Strip`, a direct Mandelbrot loop whose kernel takes about 0.1 ms, then preset). A big block the collector hands out again would not fault.
- Comparisons in single precision kernels stay two-way when an operand comes from a 32 bit member: `Perturbation.Rebased` keeps `position` (set from the member `start`) as f32 plus numerator plus denominator, the denominator is only known at runtime, so `next < start` and `next + 1 is last` each compile to a float compare, an integer compare with two multiplications and a select, and every `orbit(position)` picks its index from the float or the numerator. FractalZoomer in full HD (`./FractalBench.exe 10000 6 1920 1080 1`): 23 ms at 10,000 iterations (46 ms on the morning of 2026-11-18, before Booleans and record indices became whole numbers in kernels and `Rebased` lost its negative positions), the direct loop (mode 5) 7.9 ms, Mojo's direct kernel 5.9 ms. A member or variable that only ever holds whole numbers (a count, an index) would need its denominator known as 1 at compile time. `MlirGuards.IsWhole` already follows origins for the index of `MlirExact.Position`, the comparisons of `MlirRational.Compared` do not use it.
- `Mandelbrot.Orbit` takes about 0.45 us per iteration on the CPU (4 to 6 ms for 10,000 iterations, `./FractalBench.exe 10000 5 64 64 2`), paid for every FractalZoomer image before the kernel starts. Each step builds a new ComplexDoubleDouble and appends a Complex to the list. Cause (2026-11-18): only records of plain numbers are split into lanes (`StructLanes.Members`), ComplexDoubleDouble holds two DoubleDouble records, so the loop calls `ComplexDoubleDouble.Squared`, `+`, `High` and `SquaredLength` as real methods and every DoubleDouble operation in them allocates an instance (about 15 per step). To fix: lanes for records nested in records (`real_high`, `real_low`, `imaginary_high`, `imaginary_low`), then these methods inline like the Complex ones.
