- Serial loops (below `MlirCost.Threshold`) read every element through `strict_hoisted_read`, `strict_bits` and `strict_is_failure` and collect with `strict_list_push`: 15-36 ns per element also for `raw(index + 397) + raw(index) * raw(index + 1)`, one thread of the parallel chunk of such a loop 22 ns. `MersenneTwister.Uints` spends 17-45 ms on 900000 numbers in its chunks of 227 (the noise of one 704x1280 Moss image, 26 of the 300 ms of a fastest2steps image).
- `GpuMemory.Released` runs a full collection before it trims (50-65 ms with Moss and its text encoder loaded), programs call it between their stages: a trim of the blocks that are already free would do there.
- Moss steps on the RTX 4090 (704x1280, 115 ms, GPU busy all the time): int8 GEMMs 51 %, dense attention 19 %, the other 30 % are ten passes per layer over the 3557x3072 activations (Activated 6 %, Hadamard 6 %, Rotated 4 %, HeadScales 3 %, Gated 3 %, Modulated 3 %, values_transposed 2 %). Scales inside Rotated and Modulated would save a pass each.
- H3 Image VAE decode at 2048x2048: 81 tiles of 256 tokens one after the other take 3.5 s after a 2.9 s sample (kernels of 15-200 us): several tiles per pass as attention groups of their own (the video tiles that were tried have 1792 tokens and outgrew the L2).
- `ImageCodec.SavePng` is stb on one thread: 0.3-0.6 s at 2048x2048, 2.5 s at 4096x4096, about 10 s at 8192x8192. Strips in parallel like SaveJpeg need deflate blocks that can be joined (stb writes one final block).
- The first Moss step of every image takes 5-16 ms longer than the next ones.
